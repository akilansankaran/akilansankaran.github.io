  <a href="/rh-viz/" class="announce-banner">Read my recent remarks on Levent Alpöge's Lean formalization of a justification for the Riemann Hypothesis.</a>

  <p class="hero-bio">I think about the intersection of <b>computational and mathematical formalism</b> with the <b>applied physical sciences</b>, spanning materials, chemistry, physics, and biology. I'm working on ML research, applied translationally across scientific domains, at <a href="/lila">Lila</a> under Ben Kompa, and I'm thinking about machine-learned exchange-correlation functionals and neural-network potentials at Harvard's <a href="https://mir.g.harvard.edu/">MIR group</a>. I've conducted research efforts on defects and electronic structure at <a href="https://www.sandia.gov/">Sandia National Laboratories</a>, as well as fluid dynamics at the <a href="https://thales.mit.edu/bush/">Bush Lab at MIT</a>. I am interested in working on long-horizon problems to work towards those fleeting, discontinuous moments of discovery that have driven scientists across eras of intelligence.</p>

<p class="hero-bio">My research efforts have been honored with the <a href="https://coolidgescholars.org">Coolidge Scholarship</a>, two ISEF first-prize wins across <a href="https://www.societyforscience.org/press-release/2022-regeneron-isef-top-winners/">Mathematics</a> and <a href="https://www.societyforscience.org/press-release/high-schoolers-win-more-than-9-million-at-regeneron-isef-2024/">Chemistry</a>, the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7TOmsbAYwbQ&t=4472s">Seaborg Award</a> and Top 40 at the <a href="https://www.societyforscience.org/regeneron-sts/2025-student-finalists/akilan-sankaran/">Regeneron Science Talent Search</a>, and the <a href="https://www.societyforscience.org/blog/five-questions-with-akilan-sankaran-winner-of-the-25000-samueli-foundation-prize/">Samueli Foundation Prize.</a> </p>
  
  <p class = "hero-bio">Outside of technical scientific work, I enjoy exploring economics and political philosophy, most recently through <a href="https://www.charlesrivereconomicslabs.org/">Charles River Economics Labs</a> and Harvard's <a href="https://iop.harvard.edu/">Institute of Politics</a>. I am also an avid <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC3BUUIJfTgrYauJq0PTKlng">Classical pianist</a> and distance runner.</p>
