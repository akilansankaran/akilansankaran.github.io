<h1>ML for Science at Lila</h1>
</div>

<div class="content-container" markdown="1">

At [Lila Sciences](https://lila.ai), I work on machine learning research applied translationally across scientific domains, under Ben Kompa. My work spans the full path from experimental data to trained model, with a focus on the following:

<ul style="list-style-type: square; margin-left: 1.2em;">
  <li>I am part of the team that connects Lila's domain science groups with its AI research team, building the shared infrastructure and training pipelines through which scientific problems become learnable ones.</li>
  <li>I have contributed to large-scale mixture-of-experts model training across the stack, from inference and serving through reinforcement learning and supervised fine-tuning optimization, to the design and engineering of the harnesses and evaluations that measure what these models can actually do.</li>
  <li>I have worked on mRNA stability, building sequence models that predict mRNA half-life from untranslated-region sequence and pairing them with a differentiable RNA folding engine for property-targeted sequence design, validated against an independent folding algorithm.</li>
  <li>I am currently pursuing a small set of domain problems across the physical and life sciences, each chosen to connect laboratory infrastructure to surrogate modeling to large-scale ML training, so that a single problem can be followed from the bench to the model and back.</li>
</ul>
</div>
