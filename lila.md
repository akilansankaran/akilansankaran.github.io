At [Lila Sciences](https://lila.ai), I work on machine learning research applied translationally across scientific domains, under Ben Kompa. My work spans the full path from experimental data to trained model, with a focus on the following:

<ul style="list-style-type: square; margin-left: 1.2em;">
  <li>I am a principal member of the team that connects Lila's domain science groups with our AI research team, building the infrastructure and training pipelines to generate learnable evaluations and environments from scientific workflows.</li>
  <li>I have contributed to large-scale mixture-of-experts model training across the stack, from inference and serving through reinforcement learning and supervised fine-tuning optimization, to the design and engineering of problem-specific harnesses and simulations.</li>
  <li>I have worked on mRNA stability, building sequence models that predict mRNA half-life from untranslated-region sequence and pairing them with a differentiable RNA folding engine for property-targeted sequence design, validated against an independent folding algorithm.</li>
  <li>I am currently pursuing a small set of domain problems across the physical and life sciences, each of which is chosen to connect laboratory infrastructure to surrogate modeling to large-scale ML training.</li>
</ul>
